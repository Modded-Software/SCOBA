# REQ-BLOOD-FX: Blood feedback on damage

- R-BLOOD-1: Each time a biological unit takes damage above 0, a blood spurt plays at the unit.
- R-BLOOD-2: Ground blood splats land around the unit and fade after a few seconds.
- R-BLOOD-3: All players see the effect.
- R-BLOOD-4: Bigger hits make a bigger spurt and more splats.
- R-BLOOD-5: Non-biological units do not bleed. Self-damage does not bleed.
- R-BLOOD-6: One unit bleeds at most once per short cooldown, so fast weapons do not spam models.
- R-BLOOD-7: Models are stock SC2 assets. The operator picks the final look.
